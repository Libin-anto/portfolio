About Me

I'm an AI Engineer passionate about building practical machine learning solutions that solve real-world problems. My focus is on creating dependable model pipelines that are both maintainable and scalable.

I specialize in turning experimental ML models into reproducible, production-ready systems. Whether it's Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, or custom ML pipelines, I enjoy the challenge of making complex systems work reliably.
